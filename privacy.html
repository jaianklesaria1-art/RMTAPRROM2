The small print

Privacy

Draft for the client to check with their advisor before launch.

What this site stores

This website doesn't ask you to create an account.

Table bookings: when you tear your ticket, your booking details (name, age, mobile number, party size, date, time and any notes) are sent to us through our website host, Netlify, so our team can confirm your table. We use them only for your booking and delete them once they're no longer needed.

Party enquiries go into a WhatsApp message that you choose to send to us.

Notes and reviews: if you leave us a note on the home page, your first name, rating, words and (if you choose) your spray tag are sent to us through Netlify. We only show a note on the website if you ticked the box to allow it, and only after we've read it.

Your browser remembers a few things on your own device only: that you confirmed you're 21 or over (for 30 days), a draft of the booking form while you fill it in (until you close the tab), and that you've already seen the intro animation.

Analytics

Right now this site doesn't use analytics or advertising cookies. If we add a visitor counter later, we'll use one that doesn't identify you and update this page first.

Other services

Our fonts load from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers. Ordering happens on AirMenus, directions open in Google Maps, Instagram links go to Instagram, and messages go through WhatsApp. These open only when you choose them, and each has its own privacy policy.

Your age

We only serve people aged 21 and over, and we may ask for ID at the taproom.
