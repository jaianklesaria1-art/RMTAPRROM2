How it works

  1. Fill in your ticket. Name, age, how many of you and when.
  2. Tear off the stub. WhatsApp opens with your booking details.
  3. We confirm. The taproom team replies on WhatsApp. Then your table's ready.
  4. Tear off the stub. Your booking is saved with us straight away.
  5. We confirm. The taproom confirms your table on the number you gave.

Booking rules (time slots, how far ahead, deposits) are still to be confirmed by the taproom.
