Chapter 01

Brewed in Kandivali

Rolling Mills brews every beer at its own brewhouse in Kandivali West.

The malt comes from Weyermann in Bamberg, Germany. Lagers, wheat beers, IPAs, a stout and ciders all come out of the same tanks, then head down the road to the taproom.
